Questions

Short answers about price, setup and safety.

Ask something else
Is Tiny Systems free?

Yes. The app and the engine underneath are free and open source. Helpers use your own Claude or ChatGPT plan, or an API key.

Do I need to know Kubernetes or the terminal?

No. The app installs and looks after everything. Developers who prefer a terminal can use tiny directly.

Which AI can helpers use?

Claude Code and Codex, signed in with a subscription (Claude Pro or Max, ChatGPT Plus or Pro) or an API key.

Where does my code go?

It stays on your computers. Helpers send only what the AI model needs to the provider you chose, the same as running Claude Code or Codex yourself.

Can a helper break my computer?

Each helper runs sealed in its own sandbox, apart from the computer it's on. It can reach only the sites you allow and holds no passwords or keys.

Which computers can be gardens?

Your Mac, through a small built-in virtual machine, or any Mac or Linux computer you can log in to on your network.
