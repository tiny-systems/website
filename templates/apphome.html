Three steps, then it mostly runs itself

  1. Add a computer

    This Mac, or any Mac or Linux machine on your network. The app sets it up for you over a normal login, no terminal needed.

  2. Give a helper a task

    Describe the job in a sentence and point it at a project. It runs Claude Code or Codex, the same tools developers use every day.

  3. Answer when it asks

    When a helper hits a real decision it stops and asks, with answers you can pick in one click. You get a notification, not a surprise.

It stays yours

Helpers work on computers you own, sealed off from everything they don't need.

How it works under the hood
On your machines
Your code and your helpers' work never leave computers you control.
No keys inside a helper
Passwords and sign-in tokens stay outside, so a helper has nothing to leak.
Nothing open to the internet
Helpers reach only the sites you allow, and nothing can reach in.
Each one sealed in a sandbox
A helper runs in its own box with its own kernel, apart from the computer it's on.
